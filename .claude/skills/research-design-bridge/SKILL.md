# PolicyMemo.ai — evidence-to-design bridge

Read `research/study/approved-baseline.md`, `research/study/amendment-proposal.md`, `research/design/translation-register.md`, `AGENTS.md`.

**For each design suggestion:** list supported claim IDs; distinguish evidence and extrapolation; define observation/trace and its collection status; describe population transfer limits, alternative explanations, researcher/teacher role conflicts, coding ambiguities, and what outcome **cannot** be inferred.

**Enforcement:** no inference that interviews, student-level structured observations or questionnaires are approved. Do not upload or process real UCL participant data with AI absent explicit authorised permission. The already-approved optional artefact/consented transcript scope is not carte blanche for new recording.

**Output:** a reviewer-facing, human-approvable candidate amendment or analysis change; label `proposed / approved / rejected / blocked`. Do not modify the actual approved protocol without a separate documented ethics decision.
