# PolicyMemo.ai — methods review

Read `AGENTS.md`, `research/methods/quality-gates.md`, `protocol/eligibility.md` and the actual full text (or explicitly state no full text).

**First classify** qualitative / experimental / quasi-experimental / longitudinal / review / conceptual. Adapt appraisal questions to the design, not an unvalidated numerical quality score.

**For empirical work:** identify population and eligibility, setting, recruitment, intervention/condition, unit of analysis, measured construct, source of data, comparator, missingness, biases/confounding, null results, ethical context and specific limitations. For group work, avoid treating students as independent group outcomes. Distinguish behaviour observed from retrospective reports and post-task changes from long-term learning.

**For conceptual work:** identify definition, assumptions, contrasted traditions, explanatory reach, practical predictions and what it does not establish.

**Output:** design-level quality judgement in prose, evidence classification, exact original locators, most important alternative explanation, and explicit **transferable / context-only / unresolved** judgement subject to researcher approval. Update one source note; do not silently change Scopus screening records.
