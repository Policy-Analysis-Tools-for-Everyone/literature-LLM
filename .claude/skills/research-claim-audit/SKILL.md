# PolicyMemo.ai — claim audit

Use the project's [quality gates](../../../research/methods/quality-gates.md), [Harvard citation standard](../../../references/harvard-standard.md) and [claim record template](../../../research/methods/citation-claim-template.md). Read `AGENTS.md` first.

**Inputs required:** specific claim(s), relevant source notes, and the original article PDF or stable original source accessible during this run. If original inaccessible, stop at **not source-verified**.

**Process:** (1) separate source result, authors' interpretation, synthesis inference, and design recommendation; (2) confirm DOI, title, sample, measures, design, exact passage/table locator; (3) search for disconfirming/null evidence; (4) test population transferability and rival explanations; (5) label each link **support / qualify / contradict / not tested**; (6) record proposed revision and whether the researcher accepted it.

**Output:** concise table of claim ID, precise wording, evidential status, page/section, conflicting evidence, limitations, revision proposed and human-status field. Never manufacture page anchors or call the AI check independent peer review.

**Scope:** critical narrative review, not automatically systematic. Avoid substituting AI model error detection for enacted policy judgement.
