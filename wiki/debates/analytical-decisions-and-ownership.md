# Analytical decisions and ownership of judgement

## Core question
Who shapes a policy analyst's decisions when work is undertaken with an AI agent, and what does it mean to 'own' the eventual recommendation?

## Direct professional evidence
[Herdağdelen, Joren & Tamkin](../sources/herdagdelen-experts-judgement.md) experimentally randomised agent preferences across four defensible causal-analysis choices and a policy recommendation. **The pooled intermediate-choice flip rate was 0.72** (PDF pp. 3, 9–10). By contrast, the **preregistered adjusted direct policy shift was not statistically significant** (PDF pp. 9–10). The authors' *exploratory* result suggests upstream leans predicted downstream recommendations (PDF p. 10), which is not the same as demonstrating a registered significant effect on the final policy choice.

Importantly, observed agent-aligned decisions, stated ownership, conscious persuasion, and independent judgement are **different constructs**. The study's non-naturalistic assigned lean limits extrapolation to ordinary AI use.

## Contrasting evidence
[Wiles et al.](../sources/wiles-2026.md) demonstrate assisted performance with limited immediate unaided posttest gains, but do not experimentally isolate **who chose the analytical frame**. Thus these are complementary not conflicting studies: capability expansion and decision influence can coexist.

[Casey](../sources/casey-public-policy-teaching.md) documents student reflections on assessing policy documents, but is undergraduate-only and cannot establish postgraduate judgement development.

## Implications for PolicyMemo.ai (research hypotheses, not findings)
- Track *when* a policy problem is framed, which evidence is treated as decisive, what criteria are chosen, and how options are eliminated.
- Distinguish an AI-originated option from an **independently contested and justified** one; do not presume a student always needs to disagree with AI.
- Compare individual, peer, and AI-supported decisions; treat any human-first vs AI-first exercise as a **research design choice** that can itself alter behaviour, not a neutral measurement.
- Be alert to a memo looking analytically coherent even when consequential upstream choices have not been interrogated.

## Open questions
How do naturalistic, educational interactions differ from agent-lean manipulation? Is adopting a defensible AI suggestion an instance of expertise, deferral, or both? What observational evidence could distinguish them without intrusive monitoring?
