# Reflective practice

**Foundational sources to appraise:** Donald Schön, *The Reflective Practitioner* (1983) and *Educating the Reflective Practitioner* (1987). Full original chapters have **not yet been closely appraised** in this wiki. Do not invent chapter/page quotations.

Potential connection: professionals may examine and revise how they frame problems *during* work, through encounters with situations, colleagues and tools. This is an analytical lens to evaluate, not a demonstrated effect of PolicyMemo.ai.

## Research questions
- When does AI feedback stimulate further reflection, rather than simply provide a replacement answer?
- How do peer dialogue and teaching interventions change the role of AI?
- What would count as observing reflection, rather than relying on self-report?
