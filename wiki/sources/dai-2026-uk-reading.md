# Dai (2026) — Postgraduate metacognition in GenAI-supported academic reading

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1787647  
**Article:** *Metacognitive strategy use in GenAI-supported academic reading: a qualitative study of postgraduate students in UK higher education* (*Frontiers in Psychology*, 17).  
**Evidence:** original qualitative study, twelve postgraduate L2 students in UK higher education. **Access:** supplied 13-page PDF. **Status:** initial full-text appraisal.

## Study and findings
Interviews and retrospective reflections were thematically analysed; ChatGPT logs served as supplementary descriptive evidence rather than a continuous independent behavioural record (PDF pp. 1, 3–5). The study reports **planning, monitoring, evaluating, information management and debugging**, including source checks, correcting misunderstandings and adjusting prompts. Differences associated with language proficiency and verification behaviours were also described (PDF pp. 7–10).

## Appraisal
The postgraduate and UK contexts are close to the intended population, but participants are L2 readers from a specific educational setting, not MPA policy students. A small purposive sample, recollection and voluntary logs limit what can be claimed about enacted cognition. Without a comparison or follow-up, the study does not establish AI-caused improvement or durable transfer.

## Contribution
Identifies candidate observable practices and limitations of retrospective reporting. Compare [Yao](yao-2025-postgraduate-writing.md). Neither paper should be used as evidence that AI-led error checking alone constitutes evaluative judgement.
