# Gao & Zhang (2026) — Accountable reconstruction in doctoral AI use

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1920947  
**Article:** *Cognitive offloading and metacognitive calibration in generative AI-mediated doctoral learning: a grounded theory study of accountable reconstruction* (*Frontiers in Psychology*, 17).  
**Evidence:** original qualitative doctoral research. **Access:** supplied 17-page PDF. **Status:** provisional full-text appraisal.

## Design and empirical material
The authors report four pilot interviews, **28 formal semi-structured interviews**, **42 voluntarily supplied AI-use artefacts**, and **12 stimulated-recall discussions** (PDF pp. 1, 3–6). These are different material counts—not 84 distinct participants. They used a grounded-theory-oriented analysis with coding, comparison and saturation checking, analysing original Chinese interviews before translation of excerpts.

## Main model
The five-part mechanism proposed is **task-entry scaffolding, modular generation, metacognitive calibration crisis, critical reconstruction and research accountability** (PDF pp. 1, 8–13). Accounts describe AI-produced coherence being mistaken for achieved understanding, and occasions when learners verify, adapt, explain and defend generated material. **This is an inductively proposed process model**, not a causally tested sequence.

## Appraisal
Combining interviews, artefacts and stimulated recall offers more than simple self-reported preferences. Limitations remain: voluntarily supplied traces can be selective, recalled episodes can be reframed after the fact, and the categories are interpretive. Neither learning durability nor policy-specific transfer is established. Doctoral research practices differ from taught postgraduate seminars.

## Relevance
‘Accountable reconstruction’ may help investigate whether a student can **reconstruct and defend** an AI-originated framing, rather than merely endorse its final form. Compare [AI influence on professional analytic choices](herdagdelen-experts-judgement.md).
