## Scope rule v0.4 — prospective, not silently retrospective

- **Direct empirical learner evidence:** postgraduate students engaged in reasoning, judgement, critical reflection, feedback uptake or complex problem-solving with generative AI. Mixed student samples require separable postgraduate evidence, or remain contextual/uncertain.
- **Professional practice:** adults working as practitioners, including teachers/educators, where the paper illuminates practices of reasoning, reflection, collaborative evaluation or professional judgement. Degree status is not required for the professional strand.
- **Undergraduate-only empirical studies:** exclude from direct learner evidence. If a framework, theoretical mechanism or distinctive methodology is of specific interest, log as a *conceptual lead*, not a core empirical inclusion.
- **Outcome versus activity:** measured assisted performance, perceived learning, observed evaluation, and demonstrated independent learning remain different evidential categories.
- **Sample size:** no fixed minimum. Assess qualitative fit, data richness, analysis and relevance. SC-0116 was excluded on combined grounds, not on `n=5` alone.
- **Future screening:** apply the new postgraduate population restriction consistently across all 763 Scopus records, including the existing focused queue and prior pilot; document additional changes and researcher exceptions. Do not relabel PRISMA figures until the population recheck and exclusion audit are complete.
