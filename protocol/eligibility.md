# Review question and eligibility (researcher scope v0.4–v0.6)

**Question:** How does interaction with generative AI shape the development, exercise and justification of judgement among adult learners and practitioners undertaking complex professional tasks?

## Included for direct empirical consideration
- Postgraduate and doctoral students engaging with generative AI in learning, research or complex analytic work.
- Professionals (including teachers and educators) engaging with generative AI during situated professional practice, reflection, critique, judgement or feedback.
- Mixed cohorts **only when postgraduate evidence can be identified**; otherwise classify as contextual or population-verification pending.
- Studies with null, adverse or contradictory findings, when relevant.

## Not direct empirical evidence
- Undergraduate-only or school samples (may remain *explicitly labelled* theory/method leads).
- Pure LLM benchmark/model confidence papers, generic credibility or misinformation surveys, opinion-only adoption studies with no practice or judgement analysis.
- Improved assisted marks alone do not demonstrate learning. Nor does an immediate unassisted quiz exhaust what learning means.

## Evidence dimensions (never merge them)
1. Assisted performance or product quality
2. Enacted judgement, reflection, revision and feedback uptake
3. Reported experiences or perceived metacognition
4. Demonstrated independent learning, transfer or retained capability
5. Socially distributed practices (peers, teachers, tool, workplace routines)

## Frameworks and terminology
Reflective practice, evaluative judgement, critique and social learning are foundational traditions to appraise. **Strategy-as-practice** is a researcher-selected exploratory lead; do not claim a specific empirical paper formally uses the tradition without checking full text.
