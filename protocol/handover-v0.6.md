# PolicyMemo.ai — Claude research handover (CURRENT v0.6)

**Prepared:** 9 November 2026  
**Status:** research handover for a focused critical narrative literature review; not a systematic review. Title-and-abstract screening is AI-assisted/provisional, not final researcher-validated eligibility.

## 1. Purpose and review question

PolicyMemo.ai is an AI-supported policy reasoning tool being studied in UCL postgraduate policy teaching, with broader relevance to professional and practitioner use.

**Review question:** How does interaction with generative AI shape the development, exercise and justification of judgement among adult learners and practitioners undertaking complex professional tasks?

**Specific empirical population boundary (researcher decision, 9 November):** postgraduate/doctoral learners and practising professionals, including relevant educators. Undergraduate-only studies are excluded as *direct empirical population evidence*. Their theoretical frameworks or methodological ideas may be retained and labelled separately. Do not presume all postgraduates are experienced professionals.

Search strands: reflective/professional practice and critique; AI-supported scaffolding and feedback; judgement, delegation and responsibility; individual/peer/teacher/AI dialogue; assisted task quality versus learning/metacognition; strategy-as-practice as an **exploratory theoretical lead**, not an adopted framework.

Do not broaden into generic AI factual credibility, model-internal confidence or automatic error-detection research. Where verification is relevant as an action in human evaluative judgement, it can be considered in a bounded way.
