# Ten-claim evidence audit — cycle 1 (9 November 2026)

**Audit method:** Ten **purposively selected high-impact claims** from the current eighteen-paper synthesis were checked directly against the researcher-supplied **original full-text PDFs**. The sample spans experimental, quantitative, qualitative, group-longitudinal and conceptual sources; it is **not a random reliability estimate**, systematic review, independent second reviewer or complete verification of all eighteen papers. Page locators below are **1-based PDF pages**. *Source-checked by AI* is not *researcher-approved*.

**Decision vocabulary:** SUPPORT = original backs bounded wording; QUALIFY = core finding supported but wording/context needs correction; PROPOSITION = defensible research inference but **not** a directly measured effect; UNSUPPORTED = source fails to establish claim.

**Provenance:** Author/title/DOI tied to the original supplied article PDFs; PDFs stay outside GitHub in the controlled source library. Existing wiki source notes were secondary aids but **not treated as evidence**.

## Cross-claim synthesis: what changes the study, and what doesn't

**Strongest actionable distinction:** three observables must remain separate: **(i) output quality or assisted performance** (C01, C04, C08), **(ii) enacted reasoning and response to challenge** (C02, C03, C06, C07), and **(iii) unassisted capability, learning or retention** (only partially addressed by C01; C05 reports subjective/metacognitive measures). Neither the first nor the second automatically proves the third.

**Evidential support for a teaching design:** C03 demonstrates that an *AI-as-critic* format can produce inspectable accept/modify/reject justifications; C04 demonstrates that higher-quality feedback does not by itself ensure stronger revisions. Together they **motivate** comparing *feedback offered* with *what is changed and why*, but **do not prove this design will develop postgraduate policy judgement**.

**Evidential support for decision episodes:** C02 supports inspecting **upstream analytic choices** and C09–C10 offer a conceptual account of **judging quality and process**. A ‘consequential decision/revision episode’ is a plausible **unit of analysis proposed by this review**, not one directly measured and validated across these ten papers.

**Counterevidence preserved:** C01's small unaided-test null, C02's non-significant adjusted final recommendation, C04's lack of superior revision gains, C05's no overall difference, and C08's near-equal AUC across AI/control should all be visible in any subsequent research design rationale.

## Consequential errors/limitations requiring correction

1. **Elkhodr sampling context (C03):** eight groups were analysed from the specific Term 2 2025 assessment; not empirically sampled across five offerings. Correct source note and any synthesis that inherits this implication.
2. **Metacognition causal language (C05, C06, C08):** self-selection, group trajectories, and calibration are distinct; never say *AI eroded independent judgement* from this set.
3. **Evidence status (C09–C10):** theoretical frameworks have conceptual authority but do not establish effectiveness.
4. **Citation reliability:** source DOIs/pages above were checked **against original PDFs**. Full bibliographic author lists, journal-volume pagination and Crossref/publisher identity have **not all been independently checked**, so this is not yet a submission-ready Harvard bibliography.
5. **Scope:** the audit checks ten selected high-leverage claims, **not** all sentences or all eighteen sources; potential unsupported claims elsewhere still require further checking.
