# Evidence-claim record template

Copy this section for each material assertion made in the synthesis or a study-design recommendation.

```yaml
claim_id: "C-001"
claim_text: ""
claim_kind: "measured_finding | authors_interpretation | reviewer_inference | design_proposition"
population: ""
study_design: ""
outcome_or_construct: ""
supporting_sources:
  - doi: ""
    source_note: ""
    locator: "PDF p. x | printed p. y | table/figure"
counterevidence: []
methodological_limitations: []
alternative_explanations: []
strength_of_claim: "strong_for_this_design | conditional | provisional | unsupported"
review_status: "AI-drafted | source-checked | researcher-approved"
reviewer: ""
review_date: ""
```

**No ‘verified’ or ‘researcher-approved’ status without the specified action actually occurring.** Unverifiable DOI, unsupported statistical significance, and fake page locators must be clearly flagged. The citation’s existence is separate from its support for the claim.
