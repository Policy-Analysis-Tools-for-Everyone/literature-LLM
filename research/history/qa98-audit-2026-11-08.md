## Scope notes

The difference between judgement *in practice* and a general attitude to AI remains decisive. Surveys can supply context even where they do not measure learning. Workshop proceedings, pure model benchmarks and generic promotional discussions generally fall outside scope. Conceptual papers with direct explanatory value can be core theoretical anchors without becoming empirical evidence.

## Methodological limitation

The earlier random exclusion audit identified ten problematic labels in thirty records. This single-model recheck is not independent human dual screening, and it cannot certify the remaining 277 exclusions. Re-review that population and have a human adjudicate exclusions before fixing final screening totals. The previously verified identification trail is 825 search hits, 62 repeated hits, and 763 unique records.
