## Missing decisions before research redesign

- Choose and justify the study's **primary analytical construct**: evaluative judgement, metacognitive regulation, reflective practice, or a justified combination.
- Determine whether a **decision/revision episode** is adequately observable in *existing* approved materials; define limits where unrecorded discussion cannot be reconstructed.
- Propose a small independent second-coding protocol and negative-case sampling; do not imply agreed inter-rater reliability figures already exist.
- Specify whether and why additional interviews/observations are necessary; these are proposed and require approval.
- Document researcher–teacher conflict-of-interest mitigation and data-processing permissions for any new method.

## Research design acceptance test

For each proposed change, write an evidence-backed rationale, the exact new data needed, an alternative explanation, what the data could support, what it **cannot** support, and the actual ethics approval status.
