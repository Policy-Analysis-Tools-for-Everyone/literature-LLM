## Optional background items — separate from consent

The first interview may ask which AI platform (if any) students used **before encountering PolicyMemo.ai**, particularly while writing the formative assignment; what they asked it to do; how they evaluated it; and why they chose not to use AI if applicable. These are optional and self-reported questions, not a request for historical third-party AI chat exports. The researcher has considered asking which AI platform students have used and their prior familiarity/confidence with AI. These should be **optional, non-sensitive and justified**, and **must not be added as if the original approved study already authorised new questionnaires**. Discuss whether collecting them through interviews is sufficient.
