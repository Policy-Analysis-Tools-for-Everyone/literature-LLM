# Practice as software — Semi-structured interview guide

**Interview 1: during the module**

**Participants:** Consenting IIPP0010 postgraduate students  
**Interviewer:** Christopher Rosselot, Juan Pablo Carbonell Muñoz or Maria Nieto Rodriguez  
**Format:** Conversational, approximately 20–30 minutes

## Before beginning

Confirm the participant is happy to proceed and whether they agree to optional Microsoft Teams audio recording. Remind them that they may skip questions or stop at any time. The discussion is not an assessment of their work.

## Six core questions

1. Can you talk me through how you approached your original formative assignment, from defining your policy problem to developing your analysis?
2. What role, if any, did generative AI play in preparing your formative assignment?
3. How did you respond to the feedback you received on your formative assignment?
4. Since submitting the formative, how has your understanding of your policy problem or your approach to analysing it developed?
5. How, if at all, have you used policymemo.ai during the module, and what has that meant for the decisions you have made about your policy problem?
6. Thinking about how you work now compared with the start of the module, what feels different in how you make and justify policy decisions, if anything?

## Interviewer guidance

Use the questions flexibly and follow the conversation. Where useful, ask for a concrete example or why a student made a particular choice. Listen for the roles of tutor feedback, peers, other AI tools and policymemo.ai without assuming that any one caused a change.

The formative assignment and tutor feedback may be referred to directly only where the student has separately consented and the interviewer has authorised access. Do not ask students to share confidential or third-party information. Keep participation identities and interview materials separate from the tutor/marker until marks are finalised.

## Closing

Ask if there is anything important that has not been discussed. Thank the participant and remind them that Naeha Rashid (naeha.rashid@ucl.ac.uk) manages consent and withdrawal requests.
