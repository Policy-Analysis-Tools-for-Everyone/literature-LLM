## Recommended submission narrative (draft, for adaptation)

> The proposed changes develop the existing qualitative study in light of a critical narrative review of AI-supported postgraduate learning and professional judgement. Rather than relying exclusively on changes in student work, the revised design will examine how students assess, justify and reconsider policy-analysis choices through ordinary teaching, selected AI-supported critiques, and optional follow-up. The study will use two specified PolicyMemo.ai teaching activities rather than requiring AI in every seminar, while retaining non-AI alternatives. Proposed structured observations and two waves of independent interviews would help interpret reasoning processes otherwise unavailable from written artefacts. A later optional individual task would offer bounded evidence about the continued exercise of judgement on an unfamiliar policy problem. The analysis remains human-led; any AI-supported critique of research interpretation will occur only under institutionally authorised conditions, otherwise it will not involve student research material. The changes to recruitment, consent, study timing and data controls will be implemented only after ethics and relevant information-security approval.

**Do not describe the final package as ethics-approved or the LLM as an independent human coder.**
