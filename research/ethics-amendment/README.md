# Proposed ethics amendment pack — working drafts (reviewed 14 November 2026)

**Study:** *Practice as software: exploring the use of AI in policy reasoning and critical thinking* (IIPP0010). **Original protocol status:** approved by IIPP Local Research Ethics Committee per original plan, **not superseded yet**. **Proposed change status:** drafts only. **Prepared:** 14 November 2026.

**Researcher-selected conceptual orientation:** Model C, **policy judgement in practice**. Focus on how students make and justify decisions, how AI and peers shape those decisions, and whether later work provides meaningful evidence of change.

## Core choices now reflected in the drafts

- **Two PolicyMemo.ai-supported teaching sessions**, Strategic Triangle and Thinking Hats; leave other teaching activities without compulsory AI.
- A **small longitudinal qualitative study** combining consented student work, voluntary chat traces, proposed structured observation, independent interviews **during the module** and **after marking**, and an optional comparable individual policy task.
- **Human-first analysis** as the baseline. Any use of an LLM to critique actual participant data is **conditional on ethics, UCL information security and data-protection authorisation**; simply redacting names is not sufficient.
- A deliberate separation of **enacted judgement** from **development over time** and **AI causation**.
