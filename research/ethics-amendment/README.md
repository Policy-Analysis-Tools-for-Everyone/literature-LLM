# Proposed ethics amendment pack — working drafts

**Study:** *Practice as software: exploring the use of AI in policy reasoning and critical thinking* (IIPP0010). **Original protocol status:** approved by IIPP Local Research Ethics Committee per original plan, **not superseded yet**. **Proposed change status:** drafts only. **Prepared:** 14 November 2026.

**Researcher-selected conceptual orientation:** Model C, **policy judgement in practice**. Focus on how students make and justify decisions, how AI and peers shape those decisions, and whether later work provides meaningful evidence of change.

## Core choices now reflected in the drafts

- **Two PolicyMemo.ai-supported teaching sessions**, Strategic Triangle and Thinking Hats; leave other teaching activities without compulsory AI.
- A **small longitudinal qualitative study** combining consented student work, voluntary chat traces, proposed structured observation, independent interviews **during the module** and **after marking**, and an optional comparable individual policy task.
- **Human-first analysis** as the baseline. Any use of an LLM to critique actual participant data is **conditional on ethics, UCL information security and data-protection authorisation**; simply redacting names is not sufficient.
- A deliberate separation of **enacted judgement** from **development over time** and **AI causation**.

## Authority and remaining steps

The current original approved plan remains authoritative. The historical `ethics-application-draft.docx` proposed one seminar group, questionnaires and other elements **not carried into the subsequently approved two-group plan**. Do not use that older draft as proof of current permission. The original participant info and consent copies are dated 2025 but internally discuss the 2026/27 project; before submission verify their **actual approved version and date**, independent contact roles and committee reference.

UCL requests a description/justification for the changes, ethical risks, an updated application and changed participant-facing documents. Highlight substantive changes when transferring these drafts into the **actual UCL/IIPP form**. [UCL guidance](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/research-innovation/funding-and-support/research-ethics/after-ethical-approval).

**Blocking confirmations before submission:** actual ethics approval record and reference, staff/interviewer availability, live module and grade-release timetable, recruitment scope, exact follow-up burden, withdrawal window, legal basis/retention review, and **AI processing pathway** (default no participant data supplied to an LLM).

No participant data is kept here. These are researcher-facing drafts and are not student-facing until approved.
