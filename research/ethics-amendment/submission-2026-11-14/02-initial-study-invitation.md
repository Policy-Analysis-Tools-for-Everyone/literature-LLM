IIPP0010 / RESEARCH RECRUITMENT

Study invitation email
SUBJECT

Invitation to participate in research on policy reasoning and AI


Hi everyone,

As part of IIPP0010, we’re inviting students to participate in Practice as software, a research
study exploring how postgraduate students make and justify policy decisions while working
independently, with peers and with AI tools such as policymemo.ai.

The research will explore how students approach policy problems, respond to feedback and use
AI to support their thinking. It will also investigate how their approaches may change during
and after the module.

Participation is entirely voluntary and will not affect your teaching, feedback or assessment.
You can continue using policymemo.ai and attending all seminars whether or not you
participate.

You can choose which aspects of the research you’d like to participate in, including sharing
selected coursework or AI conversations, and potentially taking part in interviews or a later
follow-up activity.

Please read the attached Participant Information Sheet before completing the consent form:

Research participation and consent form

Consent and withdrawal requests are managed independently by Naeha Rashid
(naeha.rashid@ucl.ac.uk). Your seminar tutor or marker will not know whether you have
participated until marking has been finalised.

Thank you for considering taking part.

Best wishes,

Naeha Rashid
