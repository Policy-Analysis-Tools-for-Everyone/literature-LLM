Practice as software
Post-module research interview invitation

Subject: Invitation to follow-up interview – Practice as software

Hi,

Thank you for indicating that you would be happy to be contacted about a follow-up to the
Practice as software research study.

Now that final grades for IIPP0010 have been released, we are inviting a small number of
students to talk about how they have approached policy analysis and AI-supported work
since the module ended.

The interview will take approximately 30–45 minutes and will explore what, if anything,
has changed in how you assess evidence, consider policy options and make decisions.
Christopher Rosselot, Juan Pablo Carbonell Muñoz or Maria Nieto Rodriguez will conduct
the conversation.

You may also choose to complete a separate, ungraded 15–20-minute policy-analysis
exercise on an unfamiliar problem. This is entirely optional: you can take part in the
interview without doing the exercise.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question, and audio recording through UCL
Microsoft Teams is optional. Your responses will not affect your academic results.

If you would like to take part, please reply to this email and we will arrange a convenient
time. Please also feel free to ask any questions. You can request withdrawal of identifiable
research contributions until 30 June 2027 by contacting me.

Best wishes,

Naeha Rashid
naeha.rashid@ucl.ac.uk
