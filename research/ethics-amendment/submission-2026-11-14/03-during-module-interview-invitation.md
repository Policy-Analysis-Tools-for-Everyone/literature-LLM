Practice as software
During-module research interview invitation

Subject: Invitation to interview – Practice as software research

Hi,

Thank you for expressing an interest in participating in the Practice as software research
study.

We’re inviting a small number of IIPP0010 students to take part in a short interview
exploring how they approach policy analysis, respond to feedback and use AI tools in their
work.

The conversation will focus on your experiences during the module, including how you
developed your initial formative assignment, whether you used AI tools before
encountering policymemo.ai, and how your approach to policy reasoning may have
changed.

The interview will take approximately 20–30 minutes and will be conducted by
Christopher Rosselot, Juan Pablo Carbonell Muñoz or Maria Nieto Rodriguez,
independently of your seminar tutor and marking.

Participation is entirely voluntary and will not affect your assessment. You may decline to
answer any question, and audio recording through Microsoft Teams will be optional.

If you’re happy to participate, please reply to this email and we’ll arrange a convenient
time.

Best wishes,

Naeha Rashid
naeha.rashid@ucl.ac.uk
