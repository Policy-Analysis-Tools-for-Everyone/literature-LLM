# Recruitment and interview communications — draft v0.1

**Status:** proposed messages. To be sent **only after** amendment approval, by named independent contacts with an approved recruitment process. Participation cannot affect assessment. Avoid suggesting that declining AI use means declining education.

## A. Initial study invitation (independent sender)

**Subject: Optional research study about policy reasoning and AI in IIPP0010**

As part of IIPP0010, researchers are exploring how students make policy decisions while working independently, with others and, where they choose, with AI-supported learning tools.

The study is entirely optional. Everyone can attend all seminars and use the normal teaching resources, including PolicyMemo.ai, regardless of whether they take part. Joining or declining will not affect assessment or marks. Your seminar tutor/marker will not be informed of your participation or research responses until grades are final.

You may choose separately whether to share ordinary written work, selected AI conversations or a final memo, and whether to be contacted about interviews, observation or a later individual exercise. You do not need to agree to all of these.

Please read the approved information sheet [link], including details of privacy, withdrawal and how to ask questions. If you wish, complete the optional consent form [link]. The independent contact for questions or withdrawal is [name/role/UCL address].

## B. Mid-module interview invitation (only students who opted in)

**Subject: Optional short conversation about your policy-analysis work**

You indicated that you are happy to be contacted about the study. We are inviting a small number of students to discuss how they work through their policy problem, how they respond to feedback and how (if at all) they use AI in their analysis.

An independent interviewer would meet with you for approximately twenty to thirty minutes. You can skip any question or decline without consequence. Your tutor/marker will not have access to your identity or responses until your assessment is complete.

The interview is separate from your assessed work. Please reply to [independent contact] if you wish to take part. Recording is optional and covered by its own permission.

## C. Post-marking interview and optional task invitation

**Subject: Follow-up conversation about your policy-reasoning practice**

Thank you for considering a follow-up to the study after your IIPP0010 grades have been finalised. We would like to hear whether your approach to policy analysis or AI-supported work has changed since teaching ended.

The follow-up interview would take approximately thirty to forty-five minutes. There is also an optional short, unfamiliar policy task, which is **not graded**, allowing us to discuss how you make and justify a policy decision on a different topic. The task is optional separately from the interview.

If interested, contact [approved contact]. You may decline or withdraw in accordance with the approved information sheet [link and dates]. We will not disclose your individual responses in any teaching or assessment context.

## Sender safeguards

Use independent recruitment and contact management when marking is incomplete. Do not include non-consenting students in invitation lists built from research responses. Replace all brackets; make the approved withdrawal/retention terms consistent across invitations, sheets and forms.
