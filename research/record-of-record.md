## Research programme

**Study:** *Practice as software: exploring the use of AI in policy reasoning and critical thinking* (PolicyMemo.ai; UCL IIPP0010). Approved original study plan records a research question about how interaction with PolicyMemo.ai affects policy reasoning and when it strengthens/weakens independent analytical judgement. It is a teaching-embedded qualitative study; research participation is optional and independent from classroom access.
