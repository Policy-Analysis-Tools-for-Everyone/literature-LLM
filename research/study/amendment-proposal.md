# Proposed amendment — not approved in this repository

**Date:** November 2026. **Status:** user-discussed and draft planning only; documentary confirmation of ethics approval for any amendment has **not** been supplied or established here.

The original approved plan covers two IIPP0010 groups, optional seminar work, voluntarily shared tool conversations, final memos after marking, and non-identifying teaching notes. It does **not** authorise the following as research procedures.

## Proposed changes under development
- Expand recruitment/observation beyond the researcher's two seminar groups.
- Approx. **six to eight semi-structured student interviews**, after assessment or with safeguards preserving independence.
- Structured observations across seminar groups.
- Explicit framework addressing cognitive scaffolding, metacognition, cognitive offloading and human–AI judgement.
- Structured Week 3 exercise around **Moore's Strategic Triangle** (public value, legitimacy/support, operational capacity).
- Structured Week 4 threshold / reasoning exercise (design to be finalised).
- Possible consent-form items about which model/platform was used and participants' prior use/confidence with AI.

## Materials proposed / pending researcher approval
Revised research plan; information sheet; consent form; invitation(s); interview guide; observation protocol and schedule; Week 3 and Week 4 worksheets.

## Strict boundary
None of the above may be represented as approved or used to collect study data until approval and study documents authorise it. Even after ethics approval, check whether identifiable/pseudonymised data can be processed by any LLM; approval of interviews does not imply approval of external AI analysis.

## Literature-informed design hypotheses (not amendment claims)
Potential analytical unit: an **episode of consequential policy reasoning** — who introduces a frame/option, what criteria and evidence are invoked, what is contested/revised, and what students can subsequently justify. These are **sensitising questions**, not a settled measurement instrument or a tested effect.
