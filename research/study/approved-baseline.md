# Originally approved research design — baseline

**Original research plan:** `practice-as-software-research-plan.md` in the research archive. Status in the supplied plan: **approved by UCL IIPP Local Research Ethics Committee**. The summary below preserves its scope; consult the actual approval and documents before operational decisions. Do not treat proposed amendments as already approved.

**Project:** *Practice as software: exploring the use of AI in policy reasoning and critical thinking*.  
**Module / setting:** IIPP0010, two seminar groups, adult postgraduate learners.  
**Project dates stated:** 9 October 2026 to 30 September 2027.  
**Research question:** “How does interaction with policymemo.ai affect the development of students' policy reasoning, and under what conditions does that interaction strengthen or weaken independent analytical judgement?”

## Approved research materials and eligible data sources

- Normal seminar written work already created for teaching, offered as an optional research item.
- Selected PolicyMemo.ai conversations **voluntarily shared by students**, with material they choose removed.
- Final Personal Policy Problem memos, **only with optional consent and after marks finalised**.
- Non-identifying, group-level teaching notes, without describing named individuals.

The original plan explicitly states **no research interviews, questionnaires, or audio/video recordings** and no research use of marks/demographic data. It discusses comparing written work before/after AI use, tracing accept/adapt/reject episodes through *consented shared conversations*, and second-coder checks of a sample.

## Consent and conflicts of role

Everyone may access the teaching tool regardless of consent; non-AI peer-review alternative available. Consent is handled by designated independent colleagues. The researcher does not learn participant identity until marking is complete. Withdrawal deadline stated as **31 March 2027**. **Do not move these deadlines or rights without ethics-approved documentation.**

## Data governance

Original plan specifies UCL OneDrive/SharePoint, limited authorised personnel, pseudonymisation and a separate identity key; researcher works with pseudonymised material after marking. It explicitly says **no student research data in an AI tool for analysis**, on personal devices or in a public GitHub repo. A **private** GitHub repo is likewise *not a substitute* for the authorised UCL storage and analysis controls. No identifiable consent/form URLs or colleague contact details are reproduced here.

## Analytical intent and reporting

Qualitative comparison of problem framing, evidence use, uncertainty, policy options and judgement; student counts rather than effect sizes; a second coder examines part of the coding; analysis plan set before actual research data analysis.
